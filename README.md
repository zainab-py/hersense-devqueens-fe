# Welcome to your Lovable project
HerSense is a comprehensive women-focused wellness platform designed to bring healthcare, skincare, haircare, lifestyle management, and self-care support into one unified space.

The platform was built with the idea that women often rely on multiple apps and resources for different aspects of health and wellness. HerSense aims to simplify that experience by creating a centralized and accessible support system for everyday care and lifestyle needs.

From personal care routines to wellness tracking and health-focused support, the platform is designed to provide a more organized, personalized, and user-friendly experience.

# Features
Women’s health and wellness support

Skincare and haircare guidance

Personalized self-care recommendations

Lifestyle and wellness management

Daily care tracking systems

User-friendly and organized interface

Personalized support experience

All-in-one wellness accessibility
